Community Transport Service Operational Area by Service Provider - DAL and DATS

Click every Data Zone on the map that falls within your organisation's operational area. For each one, also indicate whether you provide DAL (Dial-a-Lift), DATS (Disability Action Transport Service), or Both there — click the zone's popup or use the buttons next to it in the list on the right. Zones without a service type set are shown in amber as a reminder. Click a zone again to remove it, or use search to find one by name. When finished, fill in your details and press Submit.

Before you submit: zoom in and out and pan across the whole map to check you haven't missed any Data Zones — it's easy to overlook one at the edge of your area or in a part of the map you haven't scrolled to.
